## 🎯 SECTION 2: QUALIFYING QUESTIONS

Use these on a WhatsApp call or in person. Be conversational, not interrogating.

### The 5 Key Questions (BANT-adapted for India):

**1. NEED — "Tamari problem samjo" (Understand their problem)**
> "Abhi aapke paas website hai ya social media page? Customers online kaise dhundh te hain aapko?"
> *(Do you have a website or social media? How do customers find you online?)*

**2. AUTHORITY — Confirm they're the decision-maker**
> "Aap hi business ke owner ho? Ya koi partner bhi hai jo isme involve hoga?"
> *(Are you the business owner? Is there a partner involved in this decision?)*

**3. BUDGET — Plant the number gently**
> "Hmara package ₹15,000 se shuru hota hai ek professional website ke liye. Aapka roughly kya budget hoga digital marketing ke liye?"
> *(Our package starts at ₹15,000 for a professional website. What's your rough budget for digital marketing?)*

**4. TIMELINE — Create urgency**
> "Kab tak aap chahte ho ki yeh ready ho jaye? Koi upcoming season, festival, ya expansion plan hai?"
> *(When do you want this ready? Any upcoming season, festival, or expansion?)*

**5. PAIN — Deepen the problem**
> "Agar ek professional website hoti aapki, toh aapka sabse bada faida kya hota? Zyada customers? Zyada trust? Ya customers khud hi call karte?"
> *(If you had a professional website, what would be your biggest benefit? More customers? More trust? Customers calling on their own?)*

### 🟢 GREEN FLAGS (Good Prospect):
- Has a real, established business
- Mentions competitors have websites
- Recently started or expanding
- Gets new customers through referrals (meaning people WANT to find them)
- Has a festive season or event coming up

### 🔴 RED FLAGS (Walk Away or Deprioritize):
- "Pehle free mein karo, phir dekhenge" (Do it free first, then we'll see)
- No clear business revenue, just starting idea stage
- Already has a nephew/cousin who "does websites"
- Wants to pay 100% after delivery only (non-negotiable)
- Cannot make decisions without 3 other people
- Asks for invoice but avoids mentioning a budget at all

## 💻 SECTION 3: DEMO SCRIPT

### What to Show (10–15 Minute Demo)

> **Goal:** Make them feel "Mujhe bhi aisi website chahiye!" (I want a website like this!)

#### Setup Before Demo:
- Have 2–3 sample websites open in browser tabs (businesses similar to theirs)
- Have your own portfolio/sample page ready on phone
- Have a "before/after" — show a bad local website vs. your clean design

---

### Demo Flow:

**Minute 1–2: Start with THEIR world**
> "Aapka business kitne saalon se hai? Aur abhi customers mainly kaise aate hain? WhatsApp se? Referral se?"

Let them talk. Then say:
> "Chaliye main aapko dikhata hoon — ek similar business ne kya kiya aur kya hua."

---

**Minute 3–6: Show a sample website**
> "Dekho — yeh ek clinic ki website hai. Jab koi Google pe 'clinic near me' search karta hai, yeh show hoti hai. Inhe har mahine 20–30 naye patients milte hain sirf website se."

Point out:
- Clean look (professional trust)
- Mobile view (sabse important!)
- Contact button / WhatsApp button
- Google Maps integration

---

**Minute 7–10: Show YOUR differentiator**
> "Lekin website banana kafi nahi hai. Main aapke Google Business Profile bhi setup karta hoon — yeh bilkul free hai Google ki taraf se. Isse aap Google Maps pe show hote ho."

Show Google Maps search live. Show how a competitor appears there.

> "Aur agar aap chahein, toh main WhatsApp automation bhi add kar sakta hoon — jab customer WhatsApp kare, woh automatically response milta hai. Aapka time bachta hai."

---

**Minute 11–13: The Money Question**
> "Sochiye — agar sirf 2 extra customers per month aaye website ki wajah se — kitna revenue hoga aapke liye?"

Let them do the math in their head. Then say:
> "Hamara ek-baar ka investment ₹[X] hai. Ek-do clients se hi yeh recover ho jata hai."

---

**Minute 14–15: Soft Close**
> "Kya aapko lagta hai yeh aapke business ke liye helpful rahega? Koi sawaal hai?"

Listen. Don't oversell. Move to proposal stage.

## 🛡️ SECTION 4: OBJECTION HANDLING

### Objection 1: "Bahut costly hai" / "Price zyada hai"
**Wrong response:** Immediately discount  
**Right response:**

> "Main samajh sakta hoon. Ek kaam karte hain — aapke business mein ek mahine mein ek naya customer average kitna deta hai? ₹2,000? ₹5,000? ₹10,000?"

*(Wait for their answer)*

> "Toh agar website se sirf 2–3 extra customers aaye pehle teen mahine mein — kya yeh investment cover nahi ho jata? Aur yeh website 5 saal se zyada chalti hai."

---

### Objection 2: "Sochna padega" / "Baad mein batata hoon"
**Wrong response:** "Haan, zaroor sochiye!"  
**Right response:**

> "Bilkul, main samajh sakta hoon. Bas ek baat — sochte waqt kaun sa hissa aapko confusing lagta hai? Price? Ya koi aur cheez?"

*(Identify the real objection, then handle that specifically)*

If they're still vague:
> "Theek hai. Main aapko ek short PDF bhejta hoon WhatsApp pe — jo sab cheez clearly explain kare. Kya kal ya parson tak hum ek baar 10 minute baat kar sakte hain?"

---

### Objection 3: "Mera beta/nephew website banata hai"
**Wrong response:** Attack the nephew  
**Right response:**

> "Yeh toh bahut achha hai! Unka kaam dekhna pasand karunga. Mera kaam thoda alag hai — main sirf website nahi banata, main Google pe aapko dhundha jaane wala setup bhi karta hoon — Business Profile, local SEO, WhatsApp automation. Unke paas yeh experience hai?"

*(Often the answer is no. Let silence do the work.)*

---

### Objection 4: "Pehle se website hai"
**Wrong response:** "Toh theek hai"  
**Right response:**

> "Bahut achha! Kya main ek baar dekh sakta hoon? Main free mein quick check karta hoon — kitna Google pe rank kar rahi hai, mobile pe kaisi dikhti hai."

*(Run a quick check — Google PageSpeed, mobile view, local search — and show them the problems you find)*

---

### Objection 5: "Guarantee kya hai? Results nahi mile toh?"
**Wrong response:** Make wild promises  
**Right response:**

> "Main results ki guarantee nahi de sakta — kyunki results aapke business pe, location pe, competition pe depend karte hain. Lekin main guarantee deta hoon: professional, mobile-friendly website, Google Business Profile live, aur aap satisfied nahi ho toh main revision karta rahunga jab tak aap happy na ho. Mere clients ke liye maine yahi kiya hai."

---

### Objection 6: "Online payment nahi karta / UPI use nahi karta"
**Right response:**
> "Koi baat nahi. Bank transfer bhi hota hai. NEFT/IMPS ka account detail main de deta hoon. Ya cash bhi chalega advance ke liye."

## 🤝 SECTION 5: CLOSING TECHNIQUES FOR INDIAN SMB CULTURE

### Principle: Build TRUST first, then ask for the order.

Indian SMB owners buy from people they trust, not from the cheapest or fanciest pitch.

---

### Technique 1: The Summary Close
> "Toh main summarize karta hoon — aapko ek mobile-friendly professional website chahiye, Google pe visible hona chahiye, aur WhatsApp se customers ka response time improve karna hai. Mere package mein yeh sab cover ho jaata hai. Kya hum aage badhein?"

---

### Technique 2: The Small Step Close
> "Toh karte kya hain — aaj 50% advance de dijiye, aur main kal se content gather karna shuru karta hoon. 10–14 din mein website live hogi. Kaise lagta hai?"

---

### Technique 3: The Festival/Urgency Close (Ethical)
> "Diwali / New Year / Season abhi aahe wali hai. Agar hum abhi shuru kare toh website ready rahegi jab sabse zyada customers search karenge. Baad mein shuru kiya toh opportunity miss ho sakti hai."

*(Only use this when there genuinely IS an upcoming event)*

---

### Technique 4: The Referral Trust Close
> "Mere ek client hain — [first name only, no fake names] — jo similar business chalate hain [City mein]. Unse ek baar baat karoge? Woh bata sakte hain unka experience kaisa raha."

*(Only offer this if you have a real satisfied client who agreed)*

---

### Technique 5: The "Just Start" Close (for Fence-Sitters)
> "Main ek suggestion deta hoon. Aaj advance na karo. Pehle mujhe apna content do — photos, business name, address, services. Main ek draft design banata hoon. Woh dekhne ke baad decide karo. Kaisa lagta hai?"

*(Low commitment entry — they're more likely to say YES once invested)*

## 📆 SECTION 6: FOLLOW-UP CADENCE

**Rule:** Fortune is in the follow-up. Most Indian SMB sales close on follow-up 3–5, not the first call.

| Day | Action | Message Type |
|-----|--------|--------------|
| Day 0 | First contact / Demo | WhatsApp message + call |
| Day 2 | Follow-up #1 | "Koi sawaal?" — soft check-in |
| Day 4 | Follow-up #2 | Share a value resource (e.g., "5 reasons businesses lose customers online") |
| Day 7 | Follow-up #3 | Final urgency nudge — "Slot available hai" |
| Day 14 | Long-term nurture | Festival greeting, industry tip, no pitch |
| Day 30 | Last check-in | "Still interested? No pressure." |

**Rule:** After 3 follow-ups with no response, move them to "Nurture" — don't spam, but keep in contact monthly.

## 💰 SECTION 7: HOW TO GET 50% ADVANCE PAYMENT

### Why 50% Advance is Non-Negotiable:
- Protects your time if client ghosts
- Signals client's seriousness
- Covers your tool costs (hosting, domain, etc.)

### How to Ask for It Naturally:

> "Mera standard process yeh hai — 50% advance pe kaam shuru hota hai, aur baki 50% delivery pe. Isse aapka bhi aur mera bhi time protect hota hai. Advance ₹[X] hai — UPI, bank transfer, ya cash — jo bhi convenient ho aapko."

### If They Push Back:

> "Main samajhta hoon hesitation hoti hai. Isliye main ek kaam karta hoon — advance ke baad main 3 din mein ek wireframe / design draft dikhata hoon. Agar bilkul pasand nahi aya, toh full refund. Iske baad toh aap khud decide karoge."

*(Reduce their risk perception — most won't ask for a refund once they see work)*

## ⚠️ SECTION 8: RED FLAGS TO AVOID

| Red Flag | What It Means | Your Action |
|----------|--------------|-------------|
| "100% payment after delivery only" | No trust, high risk of non-payment | Walk away politely |
| Negotiates before seeing proposal | Will keep negotiating forever | Set firm but flexible terms |
| "Mera dost bahut sasta karta hai" | Using you for price comparison | Ask what's different about your offering |
| Changes requirements every meeting | Scope creep risk | Lock scope in writing before starting |
| No clear contact number / hides name | Not serious | Verify before investing time |
| Wants everything "super urgent" for free | Will not value your work | Charge a rush fee |
| Asks to copy competitor's website exactly | Copyright risk, lazy client | Educate or decline |
| Pays partial and promises rest "next week" (repeatedly) | Payment risk | Stop work, politely firm |
