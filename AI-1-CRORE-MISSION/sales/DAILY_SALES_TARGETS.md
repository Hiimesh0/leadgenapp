# 📅 30-DAY SALES ACTIVITY CALENDAR
### Goal: ₹1 Lakh Collected Revenue in 30 Days
### Schedule: 4–6 Hours/Day Maximum | Health-First Approach

---

## 🔢 THE MATH THAT DRIVES THIS PLAN

```
TARGET: ₹1,00,000 collected in 30 days

Possible scenario:
  • 2 clients × ₹25,000 = ₹50,000
  • 1 client × ₹35,000 = ₹35,000
  • 1 client × ₹15,000 = ₹15,000
  TOTAL = ₹1,00,000 ✅

What it takes to get 3–4 clients:
  • Contact 50 prospects → ~20 respond → ~8 demos → ~4 proposals → ~3–4 clients

  Conversion logic:
  50 contacts × 40% response rate = 20 conversations
  20 conversations × 40% demo rate = 8 demos
  8 demos × 50% proposal rate = 4 proposals
  4 proposals × 75% close rate = 3 clients (conservative)

  Daily activity needed:
  Week 1: 0 outreach (setup only)
  Week 2: 5–8 new contacts/day = 35–50 total contacts
  Week 3: Follow-ups + 3–5 new contacts/day
  Week 4: Close + collect payments
```

---

## 📋 DAILY SCHEDULE TEMPLATE

```
Morning Block (9:00 AM – 11:00 AM) — 2 hours: Focused outreach + follow-up
Midday Break (11:00 AM – 12:00 PM) — Rest, lunch prep
Afternoon Block (12:00 PM – 2:00 PM) — 2 hours: Calls, demos, client work
Evening Block (5:00 PM – 7:00 PM) — 2 hours: Research, content, planning
```
*(Adjust to your natural energy rhythm. Don't work through fatigue.)*

## 📅 WEEK 1: FOUNDATION & VALIDATION (Days 1–7)
### Theme: Build the engine before driving it.

---

### DAY 1 — Monday | "Know Your Weapon"
**Time Needed:** 4 hours  
**Tasks:**
- [ ] Read SALES_PLAYBOOK.md fully (1 hour)
- [ ] Read OUTREACH_TEMPLATES.md fully (30 min)
- [ ] Set up LEAD_TRACKER.csv in Google Sheets or Excel (30 min)
- [ ] Create or finalize your portfolio: 2–3 sample websites to show prospects (1.5 hours)
- [ ] Write down your personal "why" — why are you doing this? Post it somewhere visible.

**Expected Output:** You know your product, your pitch, and your tools.  
**Health Note:** Take a 10-min walk between tasks. No screens after 9 PM tonight.

---

### DAY 2 — Tuesday | "Know Your Customer"
**Time Needed:** 4 hours  
**Tasks:**
- [ ] Research 3 business types you'll target: clinics, shops, CA offices, restaurants, tutoring centers (pick 3)
- [ ] For each type, write down: What problem does a website solve for them? What words do THEY use?
- [ ] Google "[business type] Ahmedabad/Surat/Vadodara" — see what comes up. Note businesses with bad/no websites.
- [ ] Create your first 15 prospect entries in LEAD_TRACKER.csv (no outreach yet — just names)
- [ ] Identify 5 businesses you can visit IN PERSON this week

**Expected Output:** 15 leads in tracker, 5 in-person visit targets.  
**Health Note:** Get outside for the research — visit some areas physically.

---

### DAY 3 — Wednesday | "Sharpen Your Tools"
**Time Needed:** 5 hours  
**Tasks:**
- [ ] Set up / verify your WhatsApp Business profile (professional photo, business description, business hours)
- [ ] Set up Google Business Profile for your OWN business (if not done)
- [ ] Prepare your "leave-behind" — a simple 1-page PDF explaining what you offer + pricing range
- [ ] Prepare printed business cards if possible (VistaPrint or local printer)
- [ ] Practice Template 1 and Template 2 from OUTREACH_TEMPLATES.md — say them out loud 5 times each

**Expected Output:** Professional-looking WhatsApp + PDF pitch sheet ready.  
**Health Note:** Don't skip meals working on setup. Eat first.

---

### DAY 4 — Thursday | "Warm Market First"
**Time Needed:** 4 hours  
**Tasks:**
- [ ] List 10 people you know personally who own or work in a business (friends, family, neighbors)
- [ ] Send them a PERSONAL message (not a template) — "Hey, I'm starting a web services business. Know anyone who needs a website?" (NOT pitching them — asking for referrals)
- [ ] Follow up on any responses — schedule conversations
- [ ] Add 10 more cold prospects to LEAD_TRACKER.csv (now at 25 total)
- [ ] Rehearse your in-person introduction script (Template 7)

**Expected Output:** 3–5 warm conversations started. 25 leads in tracker.  
**Health Note:** Warm market outreach can feel vulnerable. That's normal. Take a break after.

---

### DAY 5 — Friday | "First In-Person Visits"
**Time Needed:** 5–6 hours (includes travel)  
**Tasks:**
- [ ] Visit 3–5 local businesses in person (use Template 7 script)
- [ ] Goal: Get their WhatsApp number + permission to follow up, NOT to close a sale
- [ ] After each visit: immediately add notes to LEAD_TRACKER.csv
- [ ] Update prospect status for each person you meet
- [ ] Evening: Review what worked, what felt awkward. Write 3 learnings.

**Expected Output:** 3–5 in-person conversations. Real names and numbers in tracker.  
**Health Note:** Wear comfortable shoes. Bring water. Don't overschedule — quality over quantity.

---

### DAY 6 — Saturday | "Portfolio Polish Day"
**Time Needed:** 3–4 hours  
**Tasks:**
- [ ] Review sample websites — are they mobile-friendly? Fast? Professional?
- [ ] Take screenshots of your best sample work for WhatsApp demos
- [ ] If you don't have real client samples yet — build 1 demo website for a fictional business type (e.g., "Demo Dental Clinic") as your proof of skill
- [ ] Rest and family time — do NOT work the full day

**Expected Output:** Demo website or polished portfolio screenshots ready.  
**Health Note:** Saturday is for recovery too. Cap at 4 hours max.

---

### DAY 7 — Sunday | "Week 1 Review + Week 2 Plan"
**Time Needed:** 2 hours  
**Tasks:**
- [ ] Review LEAD_TRACKER.csv — how many leads? What's their status?
- [ ] Identify your 10 "best fit" prospects to contact first in Week 2
- [ ] Write down 3 things that went well and 3 things to improve
- [ ] Rest. Genuinely. Your brain needs it to sell well.

**Expected Output:** 25–30 leads in tracker. 10 priority targets identified.  
**Week 1 Goal Check:** ₹0 collected is expected. This was setup week.
