## PHASE 4: SCALE (Days 31–90)
**Goal:** ₹3 lakh/month revenue, build recurring income

- Hire first part-time helper or AI-assisted virtual assistant
- Systemize delivery with SOPs
- Build referral network
- Launch Google Business Profile for our own business
- Start content marketing (LinkedIn, Facebook groups)
- Target 8–10 projects per month at ₹25,000 avg
- Build retainer base: ₹15,000–₹25,000/month recurring

---

## PHASE 5: GROWTH (Days 91–180)
**Goal:** ₹5–8 lakh/month, operational stability

- Add one specialized skill (e.g., e-commerce, advanced automation)
- Consider hiring a junior developer (after ₹3+ lakh/month revenue)
- Premium pricing (₹35,000+ average)
- Regular inbound leads from website + Google
- Case studies driving trust

---

## PHASE 6: CRORE MISSION COMPLETION (Days 181–365)
**Goal:** Cumulative ₹1 crore collected

**Math to ₹1 crore:**
- Months 1–3: Average ₹2 lakh/month = ₹6 lakh
- Months 4–6: Average ₹6 lakh/month = ₹18 lakh
- Months 7–9: Average ₹10 lakh/month = ₹30 lakh
- Months 10–12: Average ₹15 lakh/month = ₹45 lakh
- **Total (conservative realistic): ₹99 lakh ≈ ₹1 crore** ✓

This requires:
- Months 1–3: ~4–6 clients/month (solo, learnable)
- Months 4–6: ~8–12 clients/month (may need 1 helper)
- Months 7–12: Team or higher-value clients (₹50k–₹1L per project)
