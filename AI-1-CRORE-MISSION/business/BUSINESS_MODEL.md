## 9-BLOCK BUSINESS MODEL CANVAS

### 1. CUSTOMER SEGMENTS
- **Primary:** Gujarat SMB owners — clinics, dental offices, CA offices, tutoring centers, retail shops, restaurants, manufacturers, trading companies
- **Geography:** Ahmedabad, Surat, Vadodara, Rajkot, Gandhinagar
- **Revenue:** ₹10L–₹5Cr/year businesses
- **Tech level:** Low-medium (smartphone users, but no website builders)

### 2. VALUE PROPOSITIONS
- "Online presence in 7 days" (speed)
- "AI-powered quality at affordable prices" (value)
- "Local team that understands Gujarat business" (trust)
- "More customers from Google" (clear ROI)
- "WhatsApp automation saves you hours" (time savings)

### 3. CHANNELS
- WhatsApp outreach (warm + cold)
- In-person visits (local commercial areas)
- Referrals from satisfied clients
- Google Business Profile (inbound)
- Facebook/LinkedIn posts
- Local business groups
- Word of mouth

### 4. CUSTOMER RELATIONSHIPS
- Personal (Kanth directly handles each client)
- WhatsApp-first communication
- Regular progress updates
- Post-delivery support
- Monthly check-ins for retainer clients

### 5. REVENUE STREAMS
- One-time project fees (₹12,000–₹60,000)
- Monthly maintenance retainers (₹2,500–₹5,000/month)
- Add-on services (chatbot, automation, SEO)
- Referral income (future)

### 6. KEY RESOURCES
- Kanth's time and skills
- AI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini, Canva — free)
- Development tools (VS Code, GitHub — free)
- WhatsApp Business (free)
- Google Workspace tools (free)
- Antigravity AI system (for planning, development, content)

### 7. KEY ACTIVITIES
- Client outreach and sales
- Website design and development
- Content creation with AI tools
- WhatsApp automation setup
- Google Business Profile optimization
- Project management and delivery
- Quality assurance and testing

### 8. KEY PARTNERSHIPS
- Google (free tools: Gmail, Drive, Analytics, Business Profile)
- GitHub (free hosting)
- Canva (free graphic design)
- Meta (WhatsApp Business — free)
- OpenAI/Google AI (content generation — free tiers)

### 9. COST STRUCTURE
- **Variable:** Kanth's time (opportunity cost)
- **Fixed:** ₹0 (all tools are free to start)
- **Optional upgrades (only when revenue justifies):**
  - Custom domain: ~₹800–₹1,500/year (Namecheap/GoDaddy)
  - Business email: ~₹150/month (Google Workspace)
  - Paid design tools: Only if needed
- **Current monthly fixed cost: ₹0**
