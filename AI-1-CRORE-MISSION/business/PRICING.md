## PRICING RATIONALE

### Why ₹12,000 minimum?
- Local freelancers charge ₹5,000–₹8,000 but deliver poorly (slow, no mobile optimization, no support)
- National platforms charge ₹3,000–₹8,000 with templates, no customization
- We deliver MORE value: AI-enhanced speed, mobile-first, SEO basics, WhatsApp button, 1-week support
- ₹12,000 is easy to justify even for a small business (less than one month's rent for most shops)

### Why not charge more immediately?
- Building portfolio — first 3–5 clients at lower end of range to get testimonials
- Once we have 3 real case studies, we can increase to ₹20,000+ for Starter
- Trust builds price power

### Market anchoring
- Always present Premium package FIRST (₹45,000–₹60,000)
- Middle package (₹25,000–₹35,000) looks like great value by comparison
- Starter (₹12,000–₹18,000) is the "affordable entry" for hesitant buyers
- This psychological anchoring increases average deal value

## FIRST 5 CLIENTS: INTRODUCTORY PRICING

For the first 5 clients (to build portfolio):

- Growth at ₹20,000 (instead of ₹25,000–₹35,000)
- In exchange: written testimonial + permission to show project in portfolio

**Script:** "Since we're showcasing your project in our portfolio, we're offering you our launch discount of ₹[X] instead of ₹[Y]. In exchange, we'd love a short testimonial after you're happy with the result."

## PRICING NEGOTIATION POLICY

- Maximum discount without CEO approval: 15%
- Below cost is never acceptable
- If client says "too expensive": offer Starter package or payment plan
- Payment plan option: 40% advance, 30% mid-project, 30% on delivery
- Never reduce scope without reducing price proportionally
