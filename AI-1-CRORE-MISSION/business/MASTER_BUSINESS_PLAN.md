## 2. THE PROBLEM WE SOLVE

Gujarat has hundreds of thousands of small and medium businesses — shops, clinics, manufacturers, trading companies, service providers — that:

1. Have no website or have an outdated website built 5+ years ago
2. Waste hours on manual tasks that AI can automate
3. Lose customers because they cannot be found online
4. Have no system to respond to leads from WhatsApp, Instagram, or Google
5. Cannot afford big digital agencies (₹1–5 lakh projects)
6. Have never heard of AI automation but feel the daily pain it would solve

**Our Mission:** Make AI-powered digital services accessible and affordable for Gujarat SMBs at a price point they can justify (₹10,000–₹50,000).
