## PART 4: REINVESTMENT vs. BUFFER STRATEGY

### Rule of Thumb: The 40-30-20-10 Split

Once personal buffer is established (from Month 3 onward):

| Allocation | % of Monthly Profit | Use |
|-----------|-------------------|-----|
| **Personal Draw** | 40% | Your salary — increase gradually |
| **Business Reinvestment** | 30% | Marketing, tools, contractors |
| **Tax Reserve** | 20% | Advance tax (quarterly) + GST (if applicable) |
| **Emergency/Savings** | 10% | FD or liquid fund |

### When to Reinvest More Aggressively

- ✅ You have 2+ months of personal expenses saved
- ✅ You have a tax reserve built
- ✅ A clear ROI opportunity exists (e.g., contractor who frees 10hrs/week)
- ✅ Paid marketing is generating measurable leads (test ₹2,000 first)

### What NOT to Do

- ❌ Do not withdraw 100% — leave business cash to handle delays
- ❌ Do not invest in tools you won't use in 30 days
- ❌ Do not spend on ads before you have a proven offer (Month 1–2)

### Income Tax

|------------------|----------|-------|

| ₹3,00,001–₹7,00,000 | 5% | New tax regime |

| ₹12,00,001–₹15,00,000 | 20% | New tax regime |
| Above ₹15,00,000 | 30% | New tax regime |

**Action Items:**
- [ ] File ITR-3 or ITR-4 (presumptive scheme u/s 44ADA for professionals)
- [ ] Under 44ADA: declare 50% of gross receipts as profit (if receipts < ₹75L) — very tax efficient
- [ ] Pay advance tax quarterly if annual tax liability > ₹10,000
  - Q1: By June 15 (15%)
  - Q2: By Sept 15 (45%)
  - Q3: By Dec 15 (75%)
  - Q4: By March 15 (100%)

### GST

- **Threshold:** GST registration required only if turnover exceeds **₹20 lakhs/year**
- **Below ₹20L:** GST registration is optional (recommended to wait)
- **Above ₹20L:** Register, charge 18% GST on services, file monthly/quarterly returns
- **Action:** Once Month 6 cumulative revenue crosses ₹15L, consult CA about GST registration timing

## PART 7: PAYMENT COLLECTION BEST PRACTICES

### Recommended Payment Methods

| Method | Best For | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| **UPI (PhonePe/GPay/Paytm)** | Advances, small amounts | Instant, zero cost |
| **IMPS/NEFT Bank Transfer** | Final payments >₹20k | Direct bank deposit, keep screenshot |
| **Razorpay Payment Link** | Clients who need invoice link | Professional, auto-receipt |
| **Cheque** | Last resort only | Takes 2–3 days to clear; avoid for advances |

### Collection Best Practices

1. **Never start work without advance** — 50% advance is non-negotiable
2. **Share payment details on WhatsApp** — make it easy with QR code
3. **Send invoice BEFORE asking for payment** — looks professional, creates paper trail
4. **Final payment due on delivery day** — include this in every proposal
5. **Follow up on Day 1, Day 3, Day 7** if payment is delayed (WhatsApp → Call → Visit)
6. **Add payment terms to every proposal:** "50% advance to begin, 50% on delivery. Work begins upon advance receipt."
7. **Use a professional invoice tool:** Zoho Invoice (free), or create a simple PDF template

### Dealing with Late Payments

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| 1–3 days late | WhatsApp reminder (polite) |
| 4–7 days late | Phone call + WhatsApp |
| 7–14 days late | Pause any retainer work; in-person follow-up |
| >14 days late | Written notice; involve community/referrer if needed |
| >30 days late | Formal legal notice via lawyer (₹500–₹1,000 cost) |

> 💡 **Pro tip:** Collect advance on WhatsApp/UPI DURING the meeting or call — before they leave. Payment rate drops sharply once client leaves.

## PART 8: EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS

### If a Month Goes to Zero Revenue

1. Do NOT panic — review pipeline first
2. Reach out to all previous clients for retainer check-in or upsell
3. Ask 5 existing clients for referrals today
4. Tap emergency fund for personal expenses (that's what it's for)
5. Run a "limited offer" — 1 discounted project to get cash flowing

### Health Emergency Fund

- Maintain ₹50,000–₹1,00,000 in liquid savings (FD or sweep account)
- Consider a health insurance policy: ₹5,000–₹8,000/year for ₹5L cover (Niva Bupa, Star Health)
- Do NOT let health costs kill business momentum — insurance is a business expense

### Family Emergency Fund

- Separate ₹1,00,000 in a savings account labeled "FAMILY EMERGENCY"
- Only use for genuine emergencies (medical, travel, crisis)
- Rebuild within 90 days if used
