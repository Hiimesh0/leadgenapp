## 8. CONCLUSION & KEY LEVERS

| Lever | Impact on Revenue |
|-------|-----------------|
| Increase avg deal size by ₹10k | +₹1.2–2L/year per 12 projects |
| Add 1 retainer client/month | +₹30–60k/year per client |
| Add 1 contractor (deliver 5 extra projects/month) | +₹18–25L/year |
| Referral program (25% client referral rate) | 2x lead volume at near-zero cost |
| Upsell retainer to 50% of clients | +₹5–8L/year |

**Bottom Line:** ₹1 crore is achievable in Year 1 but requires deliberate scaling beyond solo capacity by Month 4–5. Start Month 1 focused on landing first clients and referrals. Prioritize retainer upsells from Day 1.
