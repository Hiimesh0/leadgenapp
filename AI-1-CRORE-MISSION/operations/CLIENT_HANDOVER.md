*Simple instructions — no coding knowledge needed!*

### Option A: Ask Us to Update (Recommended for Beginners)
- **Within 30 days of delivery:** Minor updates are FREE
- **After 30 days:** Small update fee of ₹200–500 applies
- Simply WhatsApp us with: "Please change [what] to [new content]"
- We'll do it within 24 hours and confirm

### Option B: Update It Yourself (For the Brave 😊)

**Step 1: Log in to GitHub**
1. Go to **github.com** and sign in
2. Click on your repository name: **[repository-name]**

**Step 2: Edit a File**
1. Click on **index.html** (your main website file)
2. Click the **pencil icon** (✏️) on the top right to edit
3. Use Ctrl+F to search for the text you want to change
4. Make your changes carefully
5. Scroll down to "Commit changes"
6. Type a short note like "Updated phone number"
7. Click **"Commit changes"** (green button)

**Step 3: See Your Changes Live**
- Wait 2–3 minutes
- Refresh your website URL
- Your changes are live! ✅

### What You Can Safely Change:
- ✅ Phone numbers
- ✅ Email address
- ✅ Business hours
- ✅ Service descriptions (text only)
