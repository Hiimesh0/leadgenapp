## 2. 📝 Client Intake Form Questions

*Collect this information via WhatsApp chat or a simple Google Form before kickoff.*

### Section A: Business Basics
1. What is the full, official name of your business?
2. What does your business do? (Describe in 2–3 sentences)
3. How long have you been in business?
4. Where are you located? (City, area, full address for contact page)
5. What are your business hours?

### Section B: Target Customers
6. Who are your main customers? (e.g., local families, other businesses, students)
7. What problem do you solve for them?
8. What area do you serve? (Local only / Gujarat / Pan-India)

### Section C: Services & Products
9. List your main services or products (up to 10)
10. What are your prices? (Or do you prefer "Call for Quote"?)
11. What makes you different from competitors?

### Section D: Contact & Communication
12. Primary phone number (for website)
13. WhatsApp number (may be different)
14. Email address (for website contact form)
15. Current social media pages? (Facebook, Instagram, etc. — share links)
16. Do you have an existing website? If yes, share the link.

### Section E: Design Preferences
17. Do you have a brand color? (If yes, share the color code or just say "blue", "green", etc.)
18. Share 2–3 websites you like the look of (any industry is fine)
19. What feeling should your website give visitors? (Professional / Friendly / Modern / Traditional)
20. Do you prefer English, Gujarati, or bilingual content?

### Section F: Goals
21. What is the #1 goal of your website? (Get phone calls / Get WhatsApp messages / Sell products / Build trust)
22. Any special features you need? (Photo gallery / Testimonials / Map / Booking form)

## 3. 📦 Brand Assets Collection Checklist

*Send this checklist to the client on Day 1 via WhatsApp.*

### Must-Have (Required before work begins)
- [ ] **Logo file** — PNG or JPG, any size (we will resize)
- [ ] **Business name** — exact spelling as it should appear on website
- [ ] **Tagline or slogan** — if you have one
- [ ] **Phone number** — as it should appear publicly
- [ ] **WhatsApp number** — for the WhatsApp button
- [ ] **Email address** — for contact form
- [ ] **Business address** — full address for contact/map section
- [ ] **Services list** — at least 4–6 services with short descriptions
- [ ] **About Us text** — a paragraph about your business (we can help write this)

### Nice-to-Have (Makes the website better)
- [ ] **Photos** — shop front, products, team, work samples (minimum 5, ideally 10+)
- [ ] **Customer testimonials** — 3–5 real quotes with customer name and city
- [ ] **Google Business Profile link** — if already set up
- [ ] **Social media links** — Facebook, Instagram, YouTube page URLs
- [ ] **Any existing brochures or marketing materials** — for content reference

### What We Handle (Client does NOT need to provide)
- ✅ Website design and layout
- ✅ Professional copywriting and content editing
- ✅ Image optimization and resizing
- ✅ SEO basics (title tags, descriptions)
- ✅ Mobile responsive design
- ✅ WhatsApp button integration
- ✅ Contact form setup
- ✅ Hosting on GitHub Pages (free)
- ✅ Domain connection advice (if client has domain)

## 4. 📅 Project Kickoff Meeting Agenda

*15–20 minute call via WhatsApp/phone. Do within 24 hours of project start.*

```
KICKOFF CALL AGENDA — [Client Name] | [Date] | [Time]

1. INTRODUCTIONS (2 minutes)
   - Quick hello, confirm you're working with Kanth directly
   - Confirm project details and delivery date

2. REVIEW INTAKE FORM (5 minutes)
   - Go through answers together
   - Clarify anything unclear
   - Note any changes

3. ASSETS STATUS (3 minutes)
   - Confirm what assets client has ready
   - Set deadline: "Please send everything by [Date+1 day]"
   - Explain what happens if assets are delayed

4. DESIGN DIRECTION (3 minutes)
   - Confirm color preference
   - Confirm any websites they liked
   - Confirm primary goal of the website

5. COMMUNICATION PLAN (2 minutes)
   - "I'll send you updates every 2 days on WhatsApp"
   - "Day 4: I'll send you a preview link to review"
   - "Please respond to review within 24 hours to stay on schedule"

6. QUESTIONS (2–3 minutes)
   - "Any questions before we begin?"

7. CLOSE
   - "I'll WhatsApp you updates. Let's build something great!"
```

- You will receive login details and a handover document

**What "Done" Means:**
- Website is live and accessible on mobile and desktop
- All your content is on the site accurately
- Contact form and WhatsApp button are working

- You have received the handover document

## 8. ☀️ First-Day Checklist for a New Project

Use this every time a new project starts.

### Admin Setup
- [ ] Assign a Client_ID (e.g., KWS-001, KWS-002...)
- [ ] Add client to CLIENT_TRACKER.csv
- [ ] Create project folder: `projects/[Client_ID]-[BusinessName]/`
- [ ] Save payment receipt/screenshot

### Communication
- [ ] Send WhatsApp welcome message
- [ ] Send email welcome message
- [ ] Share brand assets checklist on WhatsApp
- [ ] Schedule kickoff call (within 24 hours)

### Preparation
- [ ] Create GitHub repository for the project
- [ ] Set up basic folder structure in project folder
- [ ] Review intake form answers and note key points
- [ ] Research client's industry for design inspiration

### After Kickoff Call
- [ ] Update CLIENT_TRACKER.csv with confirmed dates
- [ ] Note any special requirements or requests
- [ ] Begin collecting assets from client
- [ ] Start design/development once assets received
