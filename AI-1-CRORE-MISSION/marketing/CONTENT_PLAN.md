## CONTENT STRATEGY OVERVIEW

### Core Philosophy
Every piece of content should answer one of three questions from a Gujarat SMB owner's perspective:
1. **"Is this useful to me right now?"** → Educational content
2. **"Can I trust these people?"** → Social proof, behind-the-scenes
3. **"Should I hire them?"** → Offer/CTA content

### Content Mix (The 4-3-2-1 Rule)
- **4 parts Educational** — tips, how-tos, industry insights (builds trust, gets shared)
- **3 parts Behind the Scenes** — your process, your journey, honest updates (builds connection)
- **2 parts Social Proof** — results, testimonials, case studies (builds credibility)
- **1 part Direct Offer** — packages, pricing, limited-time CTA (drives conversions)

### Platforms & Frequency
| Platform | Frequency | Format | Primary Goal |
|----------|-----------|--------|-------------|
| WhatsApp Status | Daily (1–2 per day) | Image + text | Top-of-mind with warm contacts |
| LinkedIn | 3x per week (Mon/Wed/Fri) | Text post or carousel | B2B credibility, CA/manufacturer audience |
| Instagram/Facebook | 3x per week (Tue/Thu/Sat) | Image, Reel, or carousel | Visual audience, clinics/restaurants/shops |
| Local Facebook Groups | 3–5x per week (comment/engage) | Comments + helpful posts | Direct local lead generation |

## LOCAL FACEBOOK GROUP ENGAGEMENT STRATEGY

### Target Groups to Join (search these in Facebook Groups)
- "Ahmedabad Business Network"
- "Surat Business Group"
- "Gujarat SME / MSME Network"
- "Gujarat Entrepreneurs"
- "Rajkot Business Community"
- "[Your City] Business / Vyapar Group"
- Industry-specific: "Ahmedabad Restaurants & Food Business," "Gujarat CA Network," etc.

### Engagement Rules (to avoid being banned or ignored)
1. **Give value first, promote second** — Spend 2 weeks answering questions and sharing tips before posting any offer
2. **Never post a direct ad** in most groups — read group rules carefully
3. **Comment helpfully** on every post about websites, digital marketing, online presence, Google, WhatsApp Business
4. **Ask questions** that spark conversation ("Which businesses in Gujarat do you think have the best websites?")
5. **Share educational posts** (your LinkedIn posts, rephrased for the group)

### Weekly Group Engagement Ritual (15 min/day)
- Morning: Spend 10 min scrolling group feeds, comment helpfully on 2–3 posts
- Evening: Check if anyone asked about digital/website/Google — respond with genuinely useful info
- 2x/week: Post an educational tip directly in the group (if rules allow)
